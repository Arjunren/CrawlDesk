A LITTLE DIRECTION

From URL to insight.

Everything you need for your first thoughtful crawl.

01

Choose your starting point

Enter the full public website URL, including https://. CrawlDesk follows links within the same scheme and hostname.

Start with 50 pages and a one-second delay. Expand Crawl settings to adjust depth, timeout, page size, or excluded paths.

02

Watch the site unfold

New pages appear as they’re discovered. Pause between requests, resume when you’re ready, or stop and keep partial results.

A request already in progress finishes before pausing or stopping. CrawlDesk respects robots.txt and stops on HTTP 429 or 503.

03

Find the details that matter

Search and filter your results. Select a page to see its title, description, H1, canonical tag, image alt attributes, and internal links.

Insights flag missing metadata, multiple H1 headings, long titles, and HTTP errors. They’re review hints, not a complete SEO audit.

04

Take your findings with you

Export a snapshot as CSV, JSON, or JSON Lines. CSV is ready for spreadsheets; JSON formats preserve all page details.

Real crawl reports are saved automatically. Open Crawl history to revisit them. Sample results are fictional and aren’t added to history.

A few things to know

This crawler reads the HTML returned by the server. It doesn’t render JavaScript, sign in, or scan PDFs. Public IPs and standard HTTP/HTTPS ports are supported. Crawl sites you have permission to explore.
